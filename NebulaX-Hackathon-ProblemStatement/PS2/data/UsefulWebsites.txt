PS2 — EXTERNAL DATA SOURCES
===========================

Everything referenced in PS2_README.md section 2.4, in one place. Nothing here
needs to be used; nothing here is the only option. Section 2.5 covers what you
may and may not do with data you bring in yourself.


LTA DATAMALL  —  the primary source for this problem statement
--------------------------------------------------------------
https://datamall.lta.gov.sg

Register for a free AccountKey, then call
  https://datamall2.mytransport.sg/ltaodataservice/<endpoint>
with an AccountKey header. Responses page 500 rows at a time via $skip.

Full API documentation, version 6.8, is in this repository at
  PS2/references/LTA_DataMall_API_User_Guide.pdf

Train disruptions, station and bus crowding, bus arrivals, road works and
planned bus route changes, lift maintenance at MRT stations, historical
passenger volumes, and geospatial layers including sheltered walkways, cycling
paths and station exits. See section 2.4 for the endpoint list and for two
traps worth knowing before you start: line codes differ between endpoints, and
crowding arrives as three separate signals rather than one.


DATA.GOV.SG  —  weather, and everything else the government publishes
---------------------------------------------------------------------
https://data.gov.sg          (catalogue: https://data.gov.sg/datasets)

The real-time weather APIs need no key:
  2-hour nowcast    https://api-open.data.gov.sg/v2/real-time/api/two-hr-forecast
  24-hour forecast  https://api-open.data.gov.sg/v2/real-time/api/twenty-four-hr-forecast
  4-day outlook     https://api-open.data.gov.sg/v2/real-time/api/four-day-outlook
  rainfall          https://api-open.data.gov.sg/v2/real-time/api/rainfall

Air temperature, humidity, wind and PM2.5 sit on the same /v2/real-time/api/
base. OpenAPI specifications for the 24-hour and 4-day endpoints are in this
repository at PS2/references/ — these are interface documents, not data.

Worth searching the catalogue for: public holidays, school terms, HDB and
population data, historical ridership. All of them change how and when people
travel.


ONEMAP  —  Singapore's official mapping service
------------------------------------------------
https://www.onemap.gov.sg/apidocs/

Free with registration. Geocoding, reverse geocoding, and a routing API
covering walk, drive, cycle and public transport. Worth looking at before you
build your own routing engine.


OPENSTREETMAP  —  the required geospatial base
-----------------------------------------------
Bulk Singapore extract
  https://download.geofabrik.de/asia/malaysia-singapore-brunei.html
Targeted queries
  https://overpass-api.de          (build them at https://overpass-turbo.eu)
Routing engines that consume OSM
  https://project-osrm.org  ·  GraphHopper  ·  Valhalla
Map rendering
  https://leafletjs.com  ·  https://maplibre.org

Two conditions, both requirements rather than suggestions. Attribution: OSM is
licensed under the ODbL, so display "© OpenStreetMap contributors" wherever you
show the map or anything derived from it. And do not hammer the public tile
servers — tile.openstreetmap.org runs on donated infrastructure and its usage
policy prohibits heavy or bulk use by applications. See section 2.3.


SG MRT UPDATES  —  service notices in free text
------------------------------------------------
https://t.me/s/sgmrt

A public Telegram channel mirroring SMRT and SBS Transit announcements as they
are posted: delays, disruptions, free bus and shuttle activation, service
resumption, and planned changes such as extended operating hours.

This is an archive, not a live source. Use DataMall's TrainServiceAlerts for
live state — it is structured, official, and its Message array carries planned
changes too. What the channel gives you that the API cannot is history: on an
ordinary day AffectedSegments is empty, so over a weekend you will collect
almost no real disruption examples from the API. The channel has years of them,
which matters if you want to build or evaluate anything that reads a service
notice and turns it into advice.

Two caveats: the same notice is often posted several times with small wording
changes as it is updated, and the web view shows times but not dates.


NEA  —  weather, air quality, heat
-----------------------------------
https://www.nea.gov.sg

Feeds the walking and cycling legs of a journey. Much of it is also surfaced
through data.gov.sg.


SINGAPORE GOVERNMENT DESIGN SYSTEM
-----------------------------------
https://designsystem.tech.gov.sg

If you want the app to look and behave like a Singapore government service.
